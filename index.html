Personal Assistant

Personal Assistant is a personal assistant application used only by its owner. Its purpose is to let the owner work with their own Google account from their private assistant software:

  • read the owner's Gmail messages,
  • send e-mails from the owner's Gmail account on the owner's behalf,
  • view and manage events in the owner's Google Calendar and in calendars shared with the owner.

The application has no public user interface and is not offered to other users. This page is public and requires no login.

Privacy Policy

Personal Assistant accesses Gmail and Google Calendar data of its owner only, and only with the owner's consent. The data is used solely for the functions listed above, is stored only on the owner's private computer, is not shared with or sold to anyone, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Personal Assistant's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
